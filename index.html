The tallest Mountain in the World

Mount Everest is 8,848.86 meters tall making it the tallest mountain on Earth.

Around 800 to 1,000 people attempt to climb and summit Mount Everest each year.

Three facts about Mount Everest

  • Everest gets about 4 mm (0.16 inches) taller every year due to shifting tectonic plates.
  • The summit is covered in marine limestone, which means it was once the bottom of the sea around 450 million years ago
  • • The summit has only one-third of the oxygen found at sea level.
